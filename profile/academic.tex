\section*{Author Biography}

Paulo H. Leocadio is a Computer Sciences Engineer, Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Model (LLM) researcher, 
and Data Scientist with over four decades of experience spanning hardware engineering, systems software, enterprise platforms, 
cloud infrastructure, and applied AI research. His work bridges the full computational stack, 
from early x86-compatible hardware and embedded systems to modern large-scale distributed and autonomous AI architectures.      


His research focuses on diffusion models, transformer-based systems, agentic AI, and governance-aware deployment of artificial intelligence. 
He approaches AI as engineered infrastructure, emphasizing interpretability, durability, and real-world operational constraints. 
Leocadio is an active author and advisor, contributing to scholarly publications and institutional AI initiatives worldwide.
